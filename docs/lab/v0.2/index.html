Lab 0.2physics lab, free play

A free dissection on tissue that tents, parts, tears and bleeds. There are no steps and no prompts: you choose where to open and how to dissect, and the debrief reports what you did.

A mouse with a wheel is recommended. End the dissection with the button in the panel on the right.

Instruments

  • Maryland dissectorA click closes the jaws and a second click opens them. Closed on tissue, it holds. Opened inside loose tissue, it spreads.
  • Advanced bipolarA quick click closes or opens the jaws with no energy, so it dissects bluntly. Hold the click to coagulate what is in the jaws. Right-click or C fires the blade through closed jaws.
  • SuctionHold to aspirate blood. The tip also dissects bluntly.

Controls

  • MoveSwings the selected instrument about its port
  • WheelSlides it in and out along its shaft (two fingers on a trackpad)
  • Space or XSwitch hands. The other hand holds its position and keeps what it has grasped.
  • EscFrees the cursor for the panels. Click the picture to take the instruments again.
  • 1 to 3Change the selected hand's instrument
  • + and −, arrowsAdvance and aim the scope
  • LAnatomy labels. A structure under the peritoneum is named once you have uncovered it.

An early educational prototype with simplified anatomy and tissue behaviour. It has not been validated, it is not an assessment, and it is not for patient care. Nothing you do in it is recorded or sent anywhere.
