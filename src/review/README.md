# __NAME__

The site at https://__DOMAIN__/: a rehearsal of the total laparoscopic hysterectomy in the browser, played with a
mouse or a trackpad. This is __LABEL__, shared with colleagues for feedback before it goes to learners.

It is an early educational prototype with simplified anatomy and tissue behaviour. It has not been validated, it is
not an assessment, and it is not for patient care.

Feedback: __EMAIL__

## What is here

- `index.html`: the whole simulator, one page.
- `vendor/three.module.min.js`: three.js, the 3D library (MIT licence; its notice is in the file).
- `CNAME`: the site's domain, for GitHub Pages.

These files are built, not written by hand. Changes are made in the simulator's source and built from there; a
change made here would be overwritten by the next review round.

The page asks search engines not to list it, sets no cookies and sends nothing anywhere. It keeps its settings
(picture quality, depth mode) in the browser's own storage. Its typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts.
