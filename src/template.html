Total laparoscopic hysterectomy

With bilateral salpingectomy. Ovaries conserved.

46-year-old with heavy menstrual bleeding and fibroids after failed medical management. Mobile 10-week uterus, no previous abdominal surgery.

She is in lithotomy and Trendelenburg with a uterine manipulator and colpotomy cup in place. 10 mm umbilical scope, two 5 mm lateral ports, pneumoperitoneum at 15 mmHg. You are looking toward the pelvis: her right is on the right of the screen.

Hand control uses this device's camera. The video is analysed in your browser and is never recorded or sent anywhere. A short calibration learns directions and your pinch from your own movements, for whichever cameras you use.

With your hands

  • Move a handIts instrument follows. Both work at once.
  • Two camerasA second camera, higher and looking down, adds depth: push forward to go in
  • One cameraThe tip follows the tissue under it, so depth takes care of itself
  • PinchThumb to first two fingers: grasp and hold, seal, cut with the hook, aspirate, place a bite
  • Pinch againOn a sealed pedicle, after relaxing: fires the cut blade
  • Open hand flatClutch: that instrument holds still until you curl the hand again

With the mouse

  • MoveSwings the selected instrument about its port
  • WheelSlides it in and out along its shaft. It works only where its tip touches tissue.
  • Click, holdGrasp, seal, cut with the hook, aspirate, place a bite
  • Right-clickFires the bipolar cut blade
  • Space or XSwitch hands; the other holds its position
  • EscFrees the cursor for the panels. Click the picture to take the instruments again.

Keyboard, either way

  • 1 to 5Change the selected hand's instrument
  • W A S D, Q EUterine manipulator: tilt, deviate, push the cup
  • CCut blade
  • + and −, arrowsAdvance and aim the scope
